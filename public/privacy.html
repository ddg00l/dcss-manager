Privacy Policy — DCSS Manager

Last updated: 26 July 2026

DCSS Manager is a free browser game hosted at ddg00l.github.io/dcss-manager. This policy explains what the game does with your data. In short: it stores your game progress, optionally in your own Google Drive, and nothing else.

What we store

The only data the game handles is your save file: game progress such as heroes, currencies, upgrades and statistics. It contains no personal information, no passwords, and no payment data.

Where it is stored

  • On your device — in the browser's local storage, so your progress persists between visits.
  • In your Google Drive (optional) — only if you choose to sign in for cloud sync. The save is written to the hidden application data folder (drive.appdata scope). This folder is private to this app: it does not appear in your Drive, we cannot see your other files, and other apps cannot see ours. You can delete it at any time by revoking the app's access in your Google account settings.

Google account access

Cloud sync uses Google Sign-In and requests a single permission: drive.appdata (create and manage this app's own private configuration data in your Drive). We request no other scopes. We never access, read, or store your email, contacts, documents, or any other Drive content. Access tokens live only in your browser and are never sent to us — we operate no server.

Analytics

The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts anonymous page views without cookies and without tracking individuals across sites.

Data sharing

We do not sell, share, or transmit your data to any third party. There is no backend server; the game runs entirely in your browser and talks only to Google Drive (for your own save) when you enable sync.

Your control

  • Export your save to a file at any time from the game's Settings.
  • Sign out to stop cloud sync.
  • Revoke this app's Drive access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which removes the stored save from your Drive.
  • Clear your browser storage to remove the local save.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via the project's GitHub repository issues.
